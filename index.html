    About this prototype. Built as a conversation starter for the reunion topic “thirty indicators of a healthy civilization / democracy,” prompted by NPR’s September 15, 2026 story, “State of U.S. democracy reaches new low, report finds.” That story covers the Stockholm-based International Institute for Democracy and Electoral Assistance’s Global State of Democracy report, which found the U.S. declining on nearly half of the thirty indicators it tracks — the lowest readings in its fifty years of data — with the sharpest drops in freedom of expression, freedom of the press, credible elections, and effective parliament, and rule of law the weakest-performing area worldwide. The thirty indicators here are adapted from that framework.

    The numbers are not data. Starting scores and lever weights are illustrative, chosen to make the relationships visible and debatable. A natural committee exercise: argue about the weights — that argument is the discussion of what holds a democracy up.
